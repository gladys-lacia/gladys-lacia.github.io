Overall fit

Strong match for the Support Lead role at Northwind. Your queue-cleanup work and macro writing map directly onto the post's core duties, though the post asks for people management that your diary only touches lightly.

Strengths

  • Closed 41 stale tickets in one week, which shows ownership of backlog health.
  • Wrote the refund-request macro, which matches the post's "improve support documentation" duty.
  • Async-first habits fit a fully remote team with a four-hour overlap.

Gaps

  • The post asks for two years of team leadership. Your CV lists mentoring but no direct reports.
  • No mention of Zendesk reporting or CSAT targets, both named in the full description.
  • The post lists weekend on-call rotations that your notes rule out.

Red flags in the post

Pay is listed as "competitive" with no range, and the trial week is unpaid. Compare both against the target salary range in your notes before applying.

Suggested cover-letter angles

  1. Open with the 41-ticket cleanup and the time it took.
  2. Describe how you taught the refund macro to teammates, as leadership evidence.
  3. Ask about the on-call rotation and pay range in the first call.

Suggested next steps

Update the CV bullet about mentoring to state the number of people and the outcome, then run the Classification Analysis again to confirm the job family still fits.
