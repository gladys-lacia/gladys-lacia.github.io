As an SEO content writer, your primary goal is to create content that appeals to two distinct audiences: **human readers** and **search engine algorithms**. 

While the core principles of SEO remain the same across the board, the execution changes depending on the medium. Here is a comprehensive, step-by-step guide to optimizing blog posts, social media posts, and other types of content.

---

### Phase 1: The Foundation (Applies to ALL Content)
Before you write a single word, you must lay the groundwork.

1. **Identify the Target Audience:** Understand who you are writing for, their pain points, and what language they use.
2. **Determine Search Intent:** Why is the user searching for this? Are they looking for information (Informational), trying to find a specific website (Navigational), comparing options (Commercial), or ready to buy (Transactional)?
3. **Conduct Keyword Research:** Identify a primary keyword and 3–5 secondary/long-tail keywords. Look for Semantic keywords (LSI) that provide context (e.g., if writing about "Apple," semantic keywords might be "iPhone," "MacBook," or "fruit," "orchard" depending on the context).
4. **Analyze the Competition:** Google your target keyword. Look at the top 3 results. Note their word count, formatting, missing information, and how you can make your content 10x better.

---

### Phase 2: How to Optimize Blog Posts & Articles
Blog posts are the bread and butter of traditional SEO. 

**Step 1: Optimize the Title (H1)**
* Include your primary keyword as close to the beginning as possible.
* Keep it under 60 characters so it doesn't get cut off in search results.
* Make it clickable (use numbers, power words, or provoke curiosity).

**Step 2: Optimize the URL / Slug**
* Keep it short, clean, and readable.
* Include the primary keyword.
* Remove stop words (e.g., instead of `/how-to-bake-a-chocolate-cake`, use `/bake-chocolate-cake`).

**Step 3: Structure with Headings (H2, H3, H4)**
* Use H2s for main sections and H3s/H4s for sub-points. 
* Weave secondary keywords naturally into these headings.
* Headings help search engines understand the hierarchy of your content and allow users to skim.

**Step 4: Optimize the Body Content**
* **The Hook:** Place your primary keyword in the first 100 words (naturally).
* **Readability:** Use short paragraphs (2–3 sentences), bullet points, and bold text to break up walls of text.
* **Depth & E-E-A-T:** Ensure your content demonstrates Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. Answer the user's questions thoroughly.

**Step 5: Implement Internal and External Links**
* **Internal Links:** Link to 2–4 relevant pages on your own website. This keeps users on the site and helps search engines crawl your site architecture. Use descriptive anchor text (not "click here").
* **External Links:** Link to 1–2 high-authority, trustworthy external sites (.edu, .gov, or major publications) to back up facts or statistics.

**Step 6: Optimize Media (Images/Videos)**
* Compress image file sizes to ensure the page loads fast (crucial for SEO).
* Rename the file name before uploading (e.g., `seo-content-guide.jpg` instead of `IMG_1234.jpg`).
* Add **Alt Text** describing the image, incorporating keywords naturally. 

**Step 7: Craft the Meta Description**
* Keep it under 155–160 characters.
* Include the primary keyword.
* Treat it as ad copy—give the reader a compelling reason to click your link over the others.

### Phase 3: How to Optimize Social Media Posts
Social media platforms (TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest) operate as their own search engines today. This is known as **Social SEO**.

**Step 1: Platform-Specific Keyword Placement**
* Place relevant keywords naturally in your captions, bio, and profile name. Algorithms scan captions to categorize your content and serve it to interested users.
* *Example:* If posting on LinkedIn, instead of saying "Here's my new post," say, "If you want to master **B2B content marketing** and improve your **SEO strategy**, read this..."

**Step 2: Optimize Hashtags**
* Use a mix of broad, niche, and location-based hashtags (if applicable).
* Treat hashtags like keywords. 
* Don't overstuff; follow the platform's best practices (e.g., 3–5 highly relevant tags on LinkedIn, 3-5 on Instagram, up to 10 on TikTok).

**Step 3: Write a Strong Hook & Formatting**
* The first line of a social post is your "Title Tag." It must stop the scroll immediately.
* Use line breaks and emojis to make captions highly readable.

**Step 4: Optimize Visuals & Accessibility**
* Add Alt Text to images on LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Facebook.
* Use closed captions/subtitles on all video content (algorithms read this text to understand the video's context).
* Add text overlays on videos (like TikTok or Reels) featuring your target keywords.

**Step 5: Drive Engagement (Algorithm Signal)**
* Social SEO relies heavily on engagement. Ask a question or include a Call to Action (CTA) like "Save this post for later" or "Drop your thoughts in the comments."

### Phase 4: How to Optimize "Other" Content Types

**1. Product Descriptions (E-commerce SEO)**
* Focus heavily on long-tail keywords (e.g., "Men's waterproof hiking boots size 10" rather than just "boots").
* Highlight benefits first, features second.
* Include a FAQ section to capture "Question" based search queries.
* Encourage and feature user-generated reviews (fresh, keyword-rich content).

**2. Website Landing Pages / Service Pages**
* Optimize for commercial or transactional intent.
* Place the main keyword in the Hero section (Above the fold).
* Ensure the page loads incredibly fast.
* Include Trust Signals (testimonials, client logos) which boost conversion rates and decrease bounce rates (both indirect SEO signals).
* Work with web developers to ensure proper **Schema Markup** (code that helps search engines understand if a page is a local business, an event, a recipe, etc.).

**3. YouTube / Video Descriptions**
* **Title:** Front-load your main keyword.
* **Description:** Write a 200+ word mini-blog post in the description box. Include primary and secondary keywords.
* **Tags:** Add relevant tags.
* **Timestamps/Chapters:** Break the video into chapters with keyword-optimized titles (e.g., `01:23 - How to do Keyword Research`). These can appear directly in Google search results.
* **Thumbnail:** While not technically an SEO text element, a high CTR (Click-Through Rate) on your thumbnail tells YouTube your video is highly relevant.

---

### Phase 5: Post-Publishing Maintenance
An SEO writer's job doesn't end when they hit publish.

* **Monitor Performance:** Use Google Search Console to see what queries the page is actually ranking for.
* **Content Refreshing:** Every 6–12 months, revisit your top posts. Update outdated statistics, add new sections based on new search trends, and tweak the title tags to reflect the current year (e.g., "Best SEO Tools in 2024").
* **Repurpose:** Turn a high-ranking blog post into a Twitter thread, a LinkedIn carousel, and a script for a YouTube video. Optimize each according to Phase 3. 

By applying these steps systematically, you ensure that every piece of content you create is easily discoverable by algorithms and highly valuable to real human beings.
